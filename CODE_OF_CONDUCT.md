# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want participation in Orchid Ranker to be welcoming, respectful, and free
from harassment, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or
personal circumstances.

## Expected Behavior

Contributors and maintainers should:

- Be respectful of differing technical views and lived experiences.
- Give and receive feedback about code and ideas constructively.
- Focus discussion on the project and its users.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's private information.
- Help make the project accessible to newcomers and experienced contributors
  alike.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults or personal attacks,
discriminatory language, sexualized language or imagery, threats, deliberate
disruption of discussion, and publishing private information without consent.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, discussions, and other interactions representing Orchid Ranker.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report a concern privately to [Sam Urmian](mailto:sam.urmian@gmail.com). Do
not include credentials, personal data, or security-vulnerability details in a
public report.

Project maintainers will review reports as discreetly as practical and may
take corrective action, including a private warning, a temporary restriction
from project spaces, or a permanent ban. Retaliation against a person who
reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
